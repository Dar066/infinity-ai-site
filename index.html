Common Questions

Do I need technical skills? ▼
No. We handle all the technical setup. You just describe what you want to automate, and we build it. We'll train your team on how to use it.
How much does it cost? ▼
We don't use fixed packages. After your free automation review, you get a clear, fixed quote before any work begins, with no hidden costs.
How long does setup take? ▼
Most automations go live in 3-7 days. Simple WhatsApp bots can be deployed in 2-3 days. Complex integrations may take 2-4 weeks depending on your requirements.
Can you integrate with my tools? ▼
Yes. We connect the tools you already use, and build what's missing:

WhatsApp: WhatsApp Business API (via Twilio), auto-replies, quick-reply buttons, order and booking confirmations, reminders
Payments: JazzCash, EasyPaisa, PayFast and bank transfers, with automatic invoices and receipts
AI assistants: chatbots that answer from your own business information, lead classification and scoring, voice agents and video consultations
Automation: n8n workflows and custom Python scripts that move data between your apps automatically
Data & reports: Google Sheets, Excel, CSV and PDF reports, and secure cloud databases (Supabase / PostgreSQL)
Websites & apps: fast websites, booking forms, customer portals and admin dashboards
Hosting & security: fast, secure hosting with SSL and protection against attacks, set up and managed for you

Using something not listed? Tell us what you use. Most modern tools can be connected.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes. Your data is encrypted, access is restricted to authorised people only, and your customer information stays under your control. We never sell or share it.
